# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Everyday builders, families, freelancers, solo operators, teachers, and local organizers running modest laptops or phones who want to organize complex plans, household budgets, client workflows, and multi-session AI prompts into visual structure without monthly SaaS subscriptions, developer terminals, or losing decisions past the 25-turn context ceiling.

## Product Purpose

Aether WebOS Kit turns messy plain-English notes into an interactive visual SVG Mind-Map Floorplan, Dual-View editorial reader, structured Lexicon/Wiki/Soul knowledge base, 6-aspect + Master Bundle Rehydration Capsules (`Markdown` and `JSON v2` with live WebCrypto/FIPS 180-4 SHA-256 digests, `~N tokens` estimates, and 1-click Rehydrate/Restore), and a portable single-file offline `.html` application that preserves interactive multi-aspect capsules in airplane mode alongside a crisp `472px` transactional summary.

## Positioning

Unlike subscription cloud note apps, rigid boxed dashboard builders, or ephemeral AI chat threads that forget context after 25 turns, Aether parses plain human language directly inside the browser into navigable multi-wave structure and portable rehydration capsules with zero installs, zero logins, zero telemetry lock-in, and full single-file ownership on local disk (`Start Day 1: Free`).

## Product Principles

1. **Disordered Prose In, Crystalline Structure Out:** Every visual metaphor reflects real transformation from messy human notes into crisp dependency geometry.
2. **Respect User Attention Through Surprise & Silence:** Omit predictive progress spoilers; earn the peak through genuine contrast between horizontal wit and mirror stillness.
3. **Operate Clarity Never Wears Costumes:** Narrative sections explore sculptural form, while the live WebOS workspace steps back into clean hairline precision.
4. **Offline Ownership Is Non-Negotiable:** One click leaves the user holding a self-contained working `.html` file they control completely.
