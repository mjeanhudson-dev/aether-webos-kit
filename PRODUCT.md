## Platform

web

## Users

Everyday builders, families, freelancers, solo operators, teachers, local organizers, and privacy-minded teams running laptops, phones, or 4K workstations who want to organize complex plans, household budgets, client workflows, and multi-session AI prompts into visual structure without monthly SaaS subscriptions, developer terminals, or losing decisions past the 25-turn context ceiling.

## Product Purpose

Aether WebOS Kit turns messy plain-English notes into an interactive visual SVG Mind-Map Floorplan with rounded nodes and cubic-bezier curves, a Dual-View editorial reader, a sculpted 4-card Agenda/Soul/Lexicon/Wiki Dossier, 6-aspect + Master Bundle Rehydration Capsules (`Markdown` and `JSON v2` with live WebCrypto/FIPS 180-4 SHA-256 digests, `~N tokens` estimates, and 1-click Rehydrate/Restore), a zero-login Smart Free Quote & Wave 0..2 Scope Synthesizer (`eloquent@eloquentwin.com`), and a portable single-file offline `.html` application that runs in airplane mode alongside a crisp `472px` transactional summary.

## Positioning

Unlike subscription cloud note apps, rigid spreadsheet-caged dashboard builders, or ephemeral AI chat threads that forget context after 25 turns, Aether parses plain human language directly inside the browser into navigable multi-wave structure and portable rehydration capsules with zero installs, zero logins, zero telemetry lock-in, and full single-file ownership on local disk (`Start Day 1: Free`).

## Product Principles

1. **Disordered Prose In, Crystalline Structure Out:** Every visual metaphor reflects real transformation from messy human notes into crisp dependency geometry.
2. **Respect User Attention Through Surprise & Silence:** Omit predictive progress spoilers; earn the peak through genuine contrast between pre-impact tension, horizontal wit, and mirror stillness.
3. **Tactile Sculpted Ergonomics Over Rigid Spreadsheet Cages:** Shape every workspace container, dossier widget, and SVG node with concentric phone-widget curvature and perceptual mineral contrast.
4. **Offline Ownership Is Non-Negotiable:** One click leaves the user holding a self-contained working `.html` file they control completely.
